Title:
2026.2.5 US Market Close | Market tumbles on economic slowdown fears and tech anxiety

Description:
On February 5th, the US stock market saw major indices close sharply lower, weighed down by a combination of economic slowdown concerns following weaker-than-expected jobs data and anxiety in the tech sector over a potential AI industry shake-up. As market focus shifted from the Fed's interest rate policy to the health of the real economy, the deteriorating employment figures fueled recession fears more than they bolstered hopes for rate cuts.

Major Indices
- S&P 500: Down 1.23% - Investor sentiment soured as weaker-than-expected jobs data heightened recession concerns.
- Nasdaq: Down 1.59% - Tech stocks broadly weakened on concerns that advancements in AI could threaten the business models of existing software companies.

Key Issues
- Spreading Slowdown Fears: With December job openings falling to their lowest level since 2020 and weekly jobless claims exceeding expectations, the market began pricing in the risk of a recession, moving beyond just a cooling labor market.
- Fears of 'Creative Destruction' in AI: The launch of a new AI model by Anthropic sparked fears that it could cannibalize the revenue models of established IT consulting and Software-as-a-Service (SaaS) companies, leading to a sell-off in related tech stocks.
- Company-Specific Headwinds and Bitcoin's Plunge: Amazon fell despite strong earnings due to the announcement of a massive investment plan. A sharp drop in Bitcoin's price caused shares of related companies like MicroStrategy to plummet, further dampening overall market sentiment.
- The Tesla Debate: The stock experienced extreme intraday volatility as concerns over slowing sales in its core EV business and legal risks clashed with optimism about its future vision as an AI platform company, including its robotaxi ambitions.

Key Stocks
- Amazon (AMZN): Down over 4.4% - Despite solid Q4 earnings, the stock fell after announcing a massive investment plan of approximately $200 billion for 2026, raising concerns about future profitability.
- MicroStrategy (MSTR): Plunged over 17% - A sharp sell-off was triggered by weak Q4 earnings, which reflected the declining valuation of its holdings amid the plunge in Bitcoin's price.
- Peloton (PTON): Tumbled over 25% - The stock plummeted after reporting Q4 revenue that missed expectations, compounded by the news of its CFO's resignation.
- ServiceNow (NOW): Down over 5.7% - The stock declined amid growing concerns that AI advancements could threaten the licensing models of traditional software companies.
- Palantir (PLTR): Fell over 6.5% - As a prominent AI-related stock, its decline reflected the market's broader fears about a potential shake-up in the AI industry.
