Title:
2026.4.10 US Market Close | Market rises as ceasefire hopes overshadow geopolitical fears

Description:
On April 10, the New York stock market saw all three major indices close higher, as hopes for ceasefire negotiations emerged, overshadowing geopolitical concerns in the Middle East. Notably, the Nasdaq extended its rally for the seventh consecutive trading day.

- Dow Jones: Up 0.58% - Investor sentiment improved on ceasefire negotiation hopes.
- S&P 500: Up 0.62% - The prospect of a diplomatic solution outweighed geopolitical worries.
- Nasdaq: Up 0.83% - Continuing its seven-day rally.

Key Market Drivers
- Middle East Ceasefire Hopes: News that Israel agreed to direct negotiations with Lebanon fueled optimism for a diplomatic resolution, driving the market's gains.
- Intuit's Structural Crisis: Shares of Intuit plunged over 7% amid concerns over the IRS's expanding free tax filing program and potential regulations, drawing significant market attention.
- Persistent Inflation Concerns: Following a hotter-than-expected March Consumer Price Index (CPI), expectations for a June rate cut have receded. Market focus now shifts to the upcoming Producer Price Index (PPI) and retail sales data.
- Shift to a Data-Dependent Market: The market has entered a phase where it reacts more sensitively to individual economic indicators than to signals from the Fed, with increased volatility expected in the near term.

Stocks in Focus
- Intuit (INTU): Plunged over 7% as concerns grew over its core business model, highlighted by direct government competition and regulatory risks.
