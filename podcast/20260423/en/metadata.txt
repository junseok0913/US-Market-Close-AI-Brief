Title:
2026.4.24 US Market Close | Markets fall on Tesla's earnings shock and surging oil prices

Description:
On April 24, the New York stock market closed lower across all major indices, hit by the dual headwinds of Tesla's earnings shock and a surge in oil prices due to geopolitical risks. The tech-heavy Nasdaq saw the steepest decline as risk-off sentiment spread throughout the market.

Major Indices
- Dow Jones: Down 0.36% - Impacted by Tesla's earnings shock and rising oil prices.
- S&P 500: Down 0.41% - Weighed down by weakness in tech stocks and heightened geopolitical risks.
- Nasdaq: Down 0.89% - Directly pressured by the drop in Tesla's stock price.

Key Issues
- Tesla's Earnings Shock: Concerns over slowing demand intensified after the company reported Q1 revenue that missed market expectations and vehicle deliveries that lagged production. This dampened investor sentiment across the tech sector.
- Surge in International Oil Prices: West Texas Intermediate (WTI) crude jumped over 4.2% on news of stalled peace negotiations between the U.S. and Iran, reigniting inflation fears.
- ServiceNow Plummets: The stock plunged over 17% following news of contract delays in the Middle East. A company filing also confirmed worsening profitability and a $4 billion short-term debt risk with a six-month maturity.
- Upcoming Key Economic Data: Market focus is now shifting to next week's FOMC interest rate decision and the March Core Personal Consumption Expenditures (Core PCE) Price Index. Volatility could increase depending on the results.

Key Stocks
- Tesla (TSLA): Down over 3.5% - On Q1 revenue that missed estimates and concerns over slowing demand.
- Texas Instruments (TXN): Surged over 19% - After providing a strong earnings forecast that beat expectations.
- ServiceNow (NOW): Plunged over 17% - Highlighted risks include contract delays, deteriorating profitability, and short-term liquidity issues.
- Rivian (RIVN): Down 4.4% - Dragged down by negative sentiment in the EV sector following Tesla's news.
- Lucid (LCID): Tumbled over 9.2% - Amid weakening investor sentiment for the entire electric vehicle industry.
