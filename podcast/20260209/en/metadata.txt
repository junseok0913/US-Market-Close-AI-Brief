Title:
2026.2.9 US Market Close | Tech Rally Reignited by AI Investment Frenzy

Description:
On February 9th, the S&P 500 and Nasdaq Composite surged to new all-time highs, fueled by a frenzy of investment in artificial intelligence. News of Alphabet's $20 billion investment plan helped reignite the tech stock rally, leading the market's ascent. Software stocks, which had previously seen a correction due to concerns about the threat of AI, also staged a strong rebound.

Major Indices
- S&P 500: +0.47% - Surpassed the 6900 mark for the first time, setting a new record high.
- Nasdaq: +0.9% - Reached a new high, powered by the tech rally amid the AI investment boom.
- Dow Jones: +0.04% - Showed relative weakness as upward momentum was concentrated in AI-related tech stocks.

Key Issues
- AI Gold Rush: Alphabet's move to issue $20 billion in bonds to expand its AI data centers attracted over $100 billion in orders. This massive influx of capital into the AI ecosystem is driving the tech rally.
- Rebound in Software Stocks: A growing perception that the 'SaaSpocalypse' fears—the idea that AI would destroy existing business models—were exaggerated has led to strong bargain-hunting in deeply sold-off software stocks.
- Goldman Sachs' Dilemma: The bank's new 'Platform Solutions' business presents a conflict between growth expectations and significant challenges, including a cumulative loss of $5.2 billion over the past three years and a potential $1.7 billion legal risk, fueling ongoing debate about its future value.
- Upcoming Economic Indicators: Market attention is now focused on key inflation data set for release next week, which will provide clues on the timing of the Fed's potential rate cuts. This includes the January Consumer Price Index (CPI) on the 13th, the FOMC minutes on the 18th, and the Personal Consumption Expenditures (PCE) index on the 20th.

Key Stocks
- Microsoft (MSFT): Rose over 3%, leading the rally and confirming the market's strong confidence in companies that are implementing and scaling AI technology.
- Nvidia (NVDA): Gained 2.5% as its role as a critical hardware supplier was highlighted by the intensifying competition to build out AI infrastructure.
- Samsara (IOT): Surged more than 7%, leading the software sector's rebound as it recovered from an oversold state caused by concerns over the AI threat.
- Alphabet (GOOGL): Declined slightly. Despite news of its massive $20 billion fundraising, issues such as a trademark lawsuit with Autodesk over AI software came into focus.
- Goldman Sachs (GS): Increased by 1.57%, as expectations for future growth potential outweighed the risks of substantial losses in its new business division.
